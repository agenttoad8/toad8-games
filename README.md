# toad8-games

Short browser games from TOAD 8 Notaries & Services.

- [Help the frog home!](frog-pond/) — tap lily pads to hop a frog to the TOAD 8 dock.
- [Find the Frogs!](find-the-frogs/) — tap the frogs hiding on a notary’s desk.
- [Spot the Difference](spot-the-difference/) — tap the six changes between two pond picnics.
- [Word Scramble](word-scramble/) — tap letter tiles to unscramble notary and pond words.
- [Memory Match](memory-match/) — flip cards and match frogs, stamps, and pond treasures.
- [Help Ted Hop to the Pond!](maze/) — drag Ted through the Maze Monday path to the lily pond.
